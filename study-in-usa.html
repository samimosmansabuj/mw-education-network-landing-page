University admission process

Eight stages from shortlisting to boarding — We handle each one with you.

01

Choose intake and shortlist

US universities have January, Spring and Summer intakes. Choose your intake and shortlist universities across discipline, target and back-up categories.

02

Sit the required tests

English proficiency: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL 80–100 or Duolingo 105. Academic: SAT/ACT for bachelor's (many are test-optional), GRE or GMAT for master's and MBA.

03

Prepare your documents

Academic transcripts and certificates, Statement of Purpose, 2–4 recommendation letters, Curriculum vitae/CV, portfolio or research papers where relevant.

04

Apply online

Run the applications through the Common App or the university portal. Most applications go directly to each university. Application fees run about USD 50–75 each.

05

Scholarships and funding

Apply for merit scholarships, assistantships and fee waivers alongside the application, and arrange the bank solvency documents showing tuition plus living costs.

06

Offer letter and I-20

Accept or decline each offer, pay the enrolment deposit, and the university issues the I-20 form needed for the F-1 visa.

07

SEVIS fee and F-1 visa

Pay the USD 350 SEVIS fee, complete the DS-160 form, book the embassy appointment and attend the interview with financial and academic evidence.

08

Pre-departure

Housing, health insurance, air tickets, forex and airport pickup — plus a briefing on campus check-in and class reporting dates.

Document checklist

Passport (valid 6+ months)
All academic transcripts and certificates
IELTS / TOEFL (or Duolingo score report)
SAT / ACT / GRE / GMAT score (if required)
Statement of Recommendation
Updated CV / résumé
Bank statement and sponsor affidavit
Experience letters (if any)
Form I-20 and SEVIS fee receipt
